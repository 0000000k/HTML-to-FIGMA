Free HTML to Figma

Local only. Capture this browser window, then paste into Figma.

Free · Open source · No upload · No account

How to use
  1. Open the page you want (site or localhost).
  2. Click Capture page (or select an element).
  3. Wait for the on-page progress bar to finish.
  4. In Figma, press ⌘V / Ctrl+V.
